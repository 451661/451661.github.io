Privacy Policy

Effective September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how layoutcam (“we,” “our,” or “the App”) handles information when you use the App. We designed layoutcam to work without an account and to keep your photos and videos on your device unless you choose to share them.

1. Photos and videos

Photos, videos, and unfinished compositions you create or import are processed on your device. We do not upload, store, inspect, or use your photos and videos to train artificial intelligence models. Content is saved to your photo library only when you choose to save it and is shared only when you initiate a share action.

2. Permissions

Depending on the features you use, layoutcam may request access to:

  • Camera — to capture photos and videos.
  • Microphone — to record sound with videos.
  • Photo library — to import media and save your creations.
  • Location (optional) — to add the shooting location to new photos or location stamps when you enable that feature.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings. Disabling a permission may make its related feature unavailable.

3. Analytics, diagnostics, and advertising

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand feature usage and improve the App. It may also display advertisements using Google Mobile Ads. These services may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, device and operating system details, general location derived from an IP address, app interactions, ad interactions, and diagnostics. The information collected depends on your device settings, region, consent choices, and the services enabled in the version of the App you use.

For details, see Google’s Privacy Policy, Privacy and Security in Firebase, and How Google uses information for advertising.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We receive purchase status and product information needed to unlock features, but we do not receive your full payment card details. Apple handles payment information according to its own privacy policy.

5. Support communications

If you email us, we receive the information you provide, such as your email address, message, device model, and operating system version. We use it only to respond, troubleshoot, and improve the App. Please do not include sensitive personal information or private media unless necessary.

6. Data retention and deletion

Media stored locally remains under your control and can be deleted from the App or your photo library. Analytics, advertising, purchase, and support records are retained by us or our service providers only as needed for the purposes described above, legal compliance, fraud prevention, and service security. You may contact us with a privacy request.

7. Children

layoutcam is not directed to children under the minimum age required to consent to data processing in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than where you live. Where required, they use safeguards intended to protect transferred information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the App changes. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the effective date.

10. Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to apps50mm@outlook.com.
